WEAPONS AND TRAPS — EXAMPLE OUTCOMES

X is the attacker and Y is the target. Both must be alive and in the same
region. X must actually hold the named weapon, or rope for the new traps.
Each paragraph is an alternative outcome, rather than consecutive events.

BOW AND ARROWS

X shoots an arrow at Y, hitting Y in the shoulder. Y forces a supply crate
between them and escapes into cover. The shoulder wound is bleeding, but
Y survives the attack. Thigh and side wounds are also possible.

X fires an arrow across a clearing. It pierces Y's chest. Y collapses and
dies before reaching cover.

X shoots an arrow at Y. Y ducks behind a supply barrier; the arrow strikes
the wood, and Y escapes unharmed.

HUNTING KNIFE

X lunges with a hunting knife, stabbing Y in the thigh. Y forces a crate
between them and escapes with a bleeding wound, surviving the attack.

X surprises Y at a supply shelter and stabs Y in the chest. Y stumbles
through the doorway and dies from the wound.

Y knocks X's attacking arm aside with a branch and retreats beyond reach,
escaping the knife attack unharmed.

SPEAR

X thrusts a spear through a gap in a trail barrier, piercing Y's shoulder.
Y pushes a crate into the gap and escapes with a bleeding wound.

X braces a spear as Y charges through a narrow gap. The point pierces
Y's chest. Y collapses beside the gap and dies.

Y sidesteps the spear point and slips through a narrow passage, unharmed.

HAND AXE

X swings a hand axe as Y passes a supply rack, cutting Y across the side.
Y forces a crate between them and escapes with a bleeding wound.

X blocks a shelter exit and strikes Y in the head with a hand axe.
Y collapses; the injury is fatal.

Y ducks the swing. The axe catches in a wooden post, giving Y time to
escape unharmed while X pulls it free.

WOODEN CLUB

X swings a wooden club at Y, striking the thigh and opening a bleeding
cut against the rough edge of a supply barrier. Y escapes into cover,
surviving the attack.

X trips Y beside a trail barrier, then strikes Y in the head with a
wooden club. Y dies from the blow.

Y steps back beyond the swing and runs around a supply shelter, unharmed.

TRAPS

Rope snare: X rigs a snare beside a steep trail. Y catches an ankle in
the loop and falls over the edge. The fall kills Y and tears the rope
loose. Alternatively, Y falls against a rock and escapes with a bleeding
thigh, or spots the loop and pulls the anchor loose without injury.

Falling log: X rigs a log above a supply trail. It can strike Y's head
with fatal force, catch Y's thigh with a broken branch as Y dives away,
or miss completely when Y hears the support creak and dives clear.

Tripwire and stake: X rigs a rope across a shelter entrance. Y can trip
onto the stake and die from a chest wound, twist aside and escape with
a bleeding thigh, or spot the rope and dismantle the trap unharmed.

Wounds remain available for the existing self-bandaging and assistance
scenes. Fatal outcomes award the attacker kill credit. Triggered or
dismantled traps leave the rope on the ground for collection; they do
not remain active traps or attack an absent tribute later.
