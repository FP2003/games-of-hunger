Batch 1: weapon variety and replacement kill-and-loot wording

Seven wording variants per scenario. Names and location are examples.

KILL AND LOOT: bow and arrows
1. Alex pins Blair's weapon arm against a barrier in the Forest and wrests away the bow and arrows. Alex takes an arrow from the seized quiver and drives its point into Blair's chest. Blair dies from the wound; Alex leaves with the bow and arrows.
2. Beside a barrier in the Forest, Alex trips Blair and pulls the bow and arrows from their grip. Alex takes an arrow from the seized quiver and drives its point into Blair's chest. Blair dies from the wound; Alex leaves with the bow and arrows.
3. Blair raises the bow and arrows in the Forest, but Alex catches the weapon arm against a barrier and tears it free. Alex takes an arrow from the seized quiver and drives its point into Blair's chest. Blair dies from the wound; Alex leaves with the bow and arrows.
4. Alex catches Blair turning beside a barrier in the Forest and wrenches away the bow and arrows. Alex takes an arrow from the seized quiver and drives its point into Blair's chest. Blair dies from the wound; Alex leaves with the bow and arrows.
5. The bow and arrows catches against a barrier during a struggle in the Forest. Alex forces Blair's grip open and seizes it. Alex takes an arrow from the seized quiver and drives its point into Blair's chest. Blair dies from the wound; Alex leaves with the bow and arrows.
6. Blair backs against a barrier in the Forest. Alex traps the weapon arm there and prises away the bow and arrows. Alex takes an arrow from the seized quiver and drives its point into Blair's chest. Blair dies from the wound; Alex leaves with the bow and arrows.
7. A close struggle in the Forest ends with Alex forcing Blair against a barrier and taking the bow and arrows. Alex takes an arrow from the seized quiver and drives its point into Blair's chest. Blair dies from the wound; Alex leaves with the bow and arrows.
