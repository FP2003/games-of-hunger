NIGHT SCENES — AUTHORING EXAMPLES

X, Y, Z and W stand for distinct living tributes. Shared scenes require
everyone to be in the same region. These are examples of the added scenes;
night1.txt remains the original reference.

TAKING TURNS

X and Y agree to take turns keeping watch. X takes the first shift while Y
rests, then quietly wakes Y to trade places.

X, Y and Z decide to take turns through the night. X watches the approach
while Y and Z rest, then wakes Y for the next shift.

X, Y, Z and W agree on a watch rotation, leaving one person awake while the
others rest. X takes the first shift and quietly wakes Y to trade places.

Watch rotations require non-hostile attitudes between X and each partner.
They improve mutual trust; they do not automatically establish an alliance.

PREPARING TO SURVIVE THE NIGHT

Forest: X packs dry leaves beneath a fallen trunk, checks both ends for
tracks, and hides the entrance with branches before settling down.

Swamp: X builds a bed of reeds on raised ground and checks that the route
back to the boardwalk stays above the water.

Mountains: X chooses a recess below the exposed ridge and builds a low
stone windbreak, leaving a gap for a quick escape.

Desert: X makes a sleeping hollow behind a dune and marks the exit with
stones before darkness hides the landmarks.

Tundra: X digs into the sheltered side of a snowbank, packing snow around
the hollow to keep the wind off while leaving room to crawl out.

Center Podium: X chooses a recess beneath the steps, checks the nearest
exit, and scatters gravel across the approach to hear anyone coming.

X makes room for Y behind a low bank. They conceal the entrance together
and face opposite approaches until it feels safe to rest.

WHEN SOMETHING HAPPENS

X hears footsteps near the sleeping hollow. X stays low until the sound
fades, then moves the bedding farther from the trail.

Only during active arena weather: the weather announced earlier reaches
X's region. X leaves an exposed sleeping spot for a sheltered hollow and
waits near the entrance in case another move becomes necessary.

Only after a closure warning, during the day: X abandons plans to sleep
in the threatened region, follows the marked exit before nightfall, and
reaches the Center Podium to look for shelter. This changes X's location.
The existing evacuation scenes handle tributes still there at closure.

AFTER THE CENTER PODIUM IS RESUPPLIED

The existing Day 3 delivery gives its recipient rations and leaves water
and a blanket at the podium for others to collect.

Only for a recipient who still holds rations: X checks the sealed rations
collected from the delivery, then chooses a sleeping spot away from the
supply lanes. X keeps the food close and watches for anyone following
the delivery route. The scene uses X's current region, even after moving.

Other tributes can collect the remaining ground supplies through existing
pickup scenes. A night scene does not trigger a second delivery or claim
that an unrelated tribute visited the podium.
